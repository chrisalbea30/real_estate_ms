# Sojourn User Guide

## What this app does

Sojourn is a web app for running a small hotel, resort or serviced apartments. You record your bookings, and the app keeps your room calendar, income, expenses, cash accounts, the report for your lawyer or accountant, your budget and your partner reports up to date from that one set of records. It works in the browser on a phone, a tablet or a computer, and you can add it to your phone's home screen like a normal app.

This guide explains every screen in plain words, with step-by-step instructions. You do not need any technical knowledge.

## Getting started

### Create an account

**What it's for:** Getting your own log-in.

1. Open the app's web address in your browser.
2. On the sign-in screen, tap **Create an account**.
3. Fill in **Your name**, **Work email** and **Password** (at least 8 characters). Tap the eye icon if you want to see what you typed.
4. Tap **Create account**.

You are signed in straight away and taken to the workspace set-up form.

**Good to know**
- No confirmation email is sent. You do not need to click any link in your inbox.
- If you see "An account with this email already exists", tap **Sign in** instead.
- If someone sent you an invitation link, do not start here. Open the link they gave you (see [Join a workspace you were invited to](#join-a-workspace-you-were-invited-to)).

### Set up your workspace

**What it's for:** Telling the app about your company and your first property. It takes about a minute, and everything can be changed later in Settings.

1. After creating your account you see **Set up your workspace**.
2. Type your **Organisation / company name**.
3. Type the **First property name**. If you leave it empty, the organisation name is used.
4. Choose the **Property timezone**. The app suggests the timezone of your device.
5. Choose the **Base currency**. All amounts are stored in this currency.
6. Type the **VAT rate (%)**. It is used on guest invoices.
7. In **Rooms to create**, type how many rooms you have (0 to 200). In **Room name prefix**, type the word to put in front of the number, for example "Room" or "Villa".
8. Tap **Create workspace**.

**What the app creates for you**
- You become the **Owner** of the organisation.
- Rooms named with your prefix and a number, for example "Room 1", "Room 2".
- Two accounts, **Bank** and **Cash**.
- Four payment methods: **Cash**, **Bank Transfer**, **Card** and **Other**.

**Good to know**
- Think carefully about the base currency. It is the currency your figures are kept in.
- "Today" in the app follows the property timezone, not the clock of your phone.

### Join a workspace you were invited to

**What it's for:** Joining a company that somebody else already set up.

1. Open the invitation link you were given.
2. The screen shows **You are invited**, the organisation name, the role you will get, the email the invitation was made for, and when it expires.
3. If you do not have an account yet, tap **Create account with (your email)**, fill in the form and tap **Create account**.
4. If you already have an account, tap **I already have an account** and sign in.
5. Tap **Accept invitation**.

**Good to know**
- You must use the same email address the invitation was made for. If you are signed in with a different email, the screen tells you so. Sign out first, then open the invitation link again and sign in (or create an account) with the right email.
- Invitation links are passed on by hand, for example in a chat message or an email written by the person who invited you. The app does not send invitation emails itself.
- An invitation can be used once and stops working after it expires. If you see "Invitation not found", "This invitation was already used" or "This invitation can no longer be used", ask the person who invited you for a new one.

### Sign in and sign out

**To sign in**
1. Open the app's web address.
2. Type your **Email** and **Password**.
3. Tap **Sign in**.

**To sign out**
- On a computer: click the **Sign out** icon at the bottom of the left sidebar, next to your name.
- On a phone: tap **More** in the bottom bar, then tap **Sign out**.

**Good to know**
- Forgot your password? Password reset by email is not available at the moment. See [Questions and troubleshooting](#questions-and-troubleshooting) for what to do.
- After too many wrong attempts you may see "Too many attempts. Please wait a minute and try again."

### Install the app on your phone

**What it's for:** Putting a Sojourn icon on your home screen so it opens full screen like any other app.

**Android (Chrome)**
1. Open the app's web address in Chrome and sign in.
2. Tap the browser menu (three dots, top right).
3. Tap **Install app** (on some phones it says **Add to Home screen**).
4. Confirm.

**iPhone or iPad (Safari)**
1. Open the app's web address in Safari and sign in.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow).
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**.

**Good to know**
- The installed app still needs an internet connection.
- The icon always opens on the Overview screen.

### Finding your way around

**On a phone**
- A bar at the bottom has four tabs plus **More**. For most people the tabs are **Home** (the Overview screen), **Guests**, **Expenses** and **Revenue**. If your role cannot see one of these, another screen you are allowed to see takes its place.
- Tap **More** to open the **Menu**. It lists every screen you are allowed to see, grouped as Dashboards, Operations, Finance, Partners, Company and Admin. The **Profile** and **Sign out** buttons are at the bottom of the Menu.
- A bar at the top shows the property name and organisation name, the currency switch (if you have more than one currency) and a round badge with your initials. Tapping the badge opens the same Menu.
- Lists show as cards, one under the other. The action icons for each record are at the bottom of its card.
- Forms slide up from the bottom of the screen. Swipe inside the form to scroll. Tap the X at the top right to close without saving.

**On a computer**
- A sidebar on the left lists every screen you are allowed to see, in the same groups.
- At the top of the sidebar is a box with the property name and organisation name.
- At the bottom of the sidebar are the currency switch, the theme icon, your name and role, a person icon (**Your profile**) and the **Sign out** icon.
- Lists show as tables. The action icons are in the **Actions** column on the right.
- Forms open as a pop-up in the middle of the screen. Press the Esc key or click the X to close without saving.

**Good to know**
- If a screen is missing from your menu, your role does not allow you to see it. See [Roles and who can see what](#roles-and-who-can-see-what).
- If a button such as **Add booking** or the bin icon is missing, your role can look at that screen but cannot add, change or delete there.

### Switch the currency you see

**What it's for:** Looking at the same figures in another currency, for example for a partner who thinks in euros.

1. Find the small row of currency codes (for example **THB** and **EUR**). On a computer it is at the bottom of the left sidebar. On a phone it is in the top bar.
2. Tap the currency you want. All amounts on lists and dashboards change at once.

**Good to know**
- The switch only appears when your organisation has more than one display currency. An Owner adds them in [Settings](#settings).
- This only changes what you see. Nothing is changed in your records. Amounts are always kept in the base currency.
- Converted amounts are estimates. They use the latest exchange rates the app has (daily reference rates from the European Central Bank), not the rate on the day of each payment.
- The small dot next to the currency codes shows how fresh the rates are. Green means the rates are less than a week old. Amber means they are older, or not loaded yet. Tap the dot to fetch new rates. The app fetches at most once an hour.
- Your choice is remembered on that phone or computer only.
- Inside forms, amounts are typed and shown in the base currency (or, for an expense, the currency you choose for that expense).

### Light and dark theme

1. On a computer, click the sun, moon or screen icon at the bottom of the left sidebar. On a phone, tap **More** and tap the same icon at the top of the Menu.
2. Each tap moves to the next choice: light, then dark, then automatic (follows your device).

Your choice is remembered on that phone or computer only.

### Switch property or organisation

**What it's for:** Moving between your properties, or between companies if you belong to more than one.

1. Tap the property name (top of the sidebar on a computer, top bar on a phone).
2. The **Switch workspace** pop-up opens.
3. Under **Organisation**, tap the company you want (shown only if you belong to more than one).
4. Under **Property**, tap the property you want.

**Good to know**
- The name can only be tapped when there is something to switch to: more than one organisation, or more than one active property.
- Bookings, rooms and the occupancy calendar belong to one property. When you switch property you see that property's guests and rooms. The money totals on the Overview and Operations screens (revenue, expenses, capital, accounts) cover the whole organisation.
- The **New organisation** link at the bottom of the pop-up opens the set-up form again so you can create a second, separate company.

## The big idea in 30 seconds

You type a booking once. The app carries it everywhere it is needed.

1. **You add a booking** on the Guests screen: room, guest, dates, price, what was paid.
2. **The booking becomes revenue.** If **Add to revenue** is ticked on the booking (it is ticked by default), the app puts exactly one line on the Revenue screen for it. The line is dated on the check-out day and its amount is the booking's **Total stay**.
3. **The booking can go on the legal report.** If **Show on legal page** is ticked on the booking, it also appears on the Legal & accounting screen, which is what your lawyer or accountant looks at.
4. **Dashboards add everything up.** Overview, Operations, Budget and the Partner dashboard read the same revenue and expense records, so everybody sees the same numbers.

The two tick boxes on a booking:

| Tick box | Ticked means | Not ticked means |
|---|---|---|
| **Add to revenue** | The booking counts as income and shows on the Revenue screen. | The booking is kept for the calendar and guest records only. It is not counted as income. |
| **Show on legal page** | The booking is listed on the Legal & accounting screen and in the legal report. | The lawyer or accountant does not see this booking. |

The two tick boxes are independent. A booking can be in revenue without being on the legal page, and the other way round.

**Rules worth remembering**
- **Booking revenue can only be changed from the booking.** On the Revenue screen these lines have a **Booking** label with a small lock, and their edit and delete buttons are greyed out. To change the amount or date, edit the booking on the Guests screen. To remove it from revenue, untick **Add to revenue** on the booking.
- Revenue counts the **Total stay**, even if the guest has not paid everything yet. Money still owed shows separately as **Balance** or **Outstanding**.
- Income that does not come from a booking (a restaurant sale, a tour commission) is added by hand on the Revenue screen.
- Expenses work in a similar way: tick **Share with legal** on an expense and it appears on the Legal & accounting screen for review.
- Choosing a **Payment method** on a booking payment or an expense moves the balance of the account linked to that method, so your Accounts screen follows your cash.

## Things that work the same on every screen

Most screens show a list of records. The list always works the same way.

### Search

1. Tap the search box above the list.
2. Start typing. The list shrinks as you type.
3. Clear the box to see everything again.

### Filters

Above most lists there are small rounded buttons (for example **All**, **Upcoming**, **In-house**) and drop-down boxes (for example **All categories**). Tap one to show only matching records. The number on a button tells you how many records it holds. On many screens you can also tap a number card at the top to filter, and tap it again to remove the filter.

### Sort (computer)

1. Click a column heading that has small arrows next to it.
2. Click it again to reverse the order.

On a phone the cards keep the standard order of the screen.

### Pages

Lists show 10 records at a time. At the bottom you can see for example "1–10 of 57". Use the arrows to move between pages, or change **10 / page** to 25, 50 or 100.

### Export

**What it's for:** Getting the list out of the app as a file, to send to someone or open in a spreadsheet.

1. Set the search and filters so the list shows what you want.
2. Tap **Export** (above the list, on the right).
3. Choose **Excel (.xlsx)**, **CSV**, **JSON** or **Print / PDF**.

**Good to know**
- The export contains exactly the records currently shown by your search and filters, on all pages, not only the 10 you can see.
- **Print / PDF** opens a new browser tab with a plain table and your printer window. Choose "Save as PDF" in the printer window to get a PDF. If nothing opens, allow pop-ups for the app in your browser.
- Amounts in exported files are in the base currency, whatever currency you are viewing on screen.
- **Export** is greyed out when the list is empty.
- Excel and CSV are the two formats a spreadsheet opens. JSON is for technical people and can be ignored.

### Add, edit and delete

- **Add:** tap the button at the top right of the screen (for example **Add booking**). A form opens. Fields marked as required must be filled in. Tap the save button at the bottom.
- **Edit:** tap the pencil icon (**Edit**) on the record. The same form opens with the details filled in.
- **Delete:** tap the bin icon (**Delete**). The app always asks you to confirm. Tap **Delete** to go ahead or **Cancel** to keep the record. Deleting cannot be undone.
- If something is wrong, a red message appears at the top of the form or under the field. Nothing is saved until the message is gone and you tap save again.
- A short message at the edge of the screen confirms each save, for example "Booking added".

### Attach a file

1. In the form, find the attachment field and tap **Choose file**. On a phone you can also tap **Camera** to take a photo on the spot.
2. Pick the file. Wait for the message "File uploaded".
3. Save the form. The file is only linked to the record when you save.

**Good to know**
- One file can be up to 10 MB.
- Photos, PDFs, Word, Excel and PowerPoint files, text files, audio and video are accepted. Other types show "File type not allowed".
- To replace a file, tap the bin icon next to it (**Remove**), then choose a new one.
- To look at a file, tap the open icon next to it, or the paperclip on the record in the list.

## Part 1: Dashboards

### Overview

**What it's for:** A one-page picture of the business: money in, money out, cash, who is staying now and who is arriving.

**Where to find it:** Computer: left sidebar, **Dashboards**, **Overview**. Phone: the **Home** tab in the bottom bar.

#### What you see

**The four cards at the top**

| Card | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Total revenue** | All income on the Revenue screen for the chosen years. |
| **Total expenses** | All expenses for the chosen years, in the base currency. |
| **Capital funded** | Money the partners have put into the company (from the Capital screen). |
| **Net position** | Revenue plus capital minus expenses. Green when it is zero or more, red when it is negative. |

**Revenue vs expenses (year)**
A bar chart with two bars per month: revenue and expenses. Below the chart is the total for the year.

**Cash position**
- **Total cash on hand:** all your active accounts added together.
- **No invoice / receipt:** the total of all expenses that have no file attached, and how many there are. It is a reminder of paperwork you still need to collect. This card always covers all years.
- One card per account with its balance, the number of money movements ("mov.") and when the last movement happened. A balance below zero is shown in red.

**In-house now**
Guests staying tonight, with room, check-out date and what they still owe. "balance due" in red means money is still owed. "settled" in green means nothing is owed.

**Arriving in 7 days**
Bookings that start in the coming days (not counting today), with the check-in date and the total price. The number of days can be changed in Settings.

**Tasks**
How many tasks are in each status: Pending, Ongoing, Complete and Blocked, across the whole organisation.

#### Choose which years to look at
1. At the top right, tap a year button, for example **2026**. The four top cards now show that year only.
2. Tap another year to add it. Tap a selected year again to remove it.
3. Tap **All** to go back to everything.

**Good to know**
- The year buttons only change the four top cards and the chart. Cash, guests and tasks are always "now".
- The chart shows one year: the year you picked if you picked exactly one, otherwise the current year.
- With **All** selected, records that have no date are counted too.

#### Read the chart
1. Point at a month (computer) or tap it (phone) to see that month's revenue and expenses under the chart.
2. Tap **Show table** to see the twelve months as numbers with a **Net** column. Tap **Show chart** to go back.

#### Jump to the details
- Tap **Accounts**, **Guests** or **Tasks** next to a section title to open that screen.
- Tap the **No invoice / receipt** card to open Expenses.
- Tap a task status card to open Tasks.

These shortcuts only appear if your role can see the screen they lead to.

**Good to know**
- Nothing can be edited on the Overview. To change an account balance, go to [Accounts](#accounts).

### Operations

**What it's for:** The manager's daily working screen: today's arrivals and departures, safety alerts, money to collect, overdue tasks and cash.

**Where to find it:** Computer: left sidebar, **Dashboards**, **Operations**. Phone: **More**, then **Operations**.

The screen has five tabs. A number on a tab tells you how many things need attention there. Nothing is edited on this screen. It gathers information and gives you shortcut buttons (**Guests**, **Tasks**, **Rooms**, **Accounts**, **Expenses**) to the screens where you make changes.

If your organisation has saved useful web links in Settings (for example the immigration registration website), they appear as buttons at the top right and open in a new tab.

#### Tab: Today's ops

| Card | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Check-ins today** | Bookings whose check-in date is today. |
| **Check-outs today** | Bookings whose check-out date is today. |
| **In-house** | Bookings staying tonight, and how many rooms that is. |
| **Available** | Active rooms that are free tonight, and tonight's occupancy as a percentage. |

Below the cards:
- **Arriving today:** each arrival with nights, booking source, number of guests ("pax") and whether the TM30 immigration notification is filed or pending.
- **Departing today:** each departure with the total and either the amount still due or a **Paid** label.
- **TM30 pending:** in-house guests whose booking does not have the TM30 box ticked yet.
- **Fire safety:** fire extinguishers that have expired or will expire soon (within 30 days unless changed in Settings), with the room and the days left.

#### Tab: Bookings
- **Arriving in 14 days:** upcoming arrivals with date, nights, source and total. A **TM30 pending** label shows where the notification is not filed.
- **Departing in 7 days:** guests leaving soon, with the balance to collect before they go.

#### Tab: Revenue & collection

| Card | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Revenue this month** | Income on the Revenue screen dated in the current month. |
| **Outstanding** | Money guests still owe (total stay minus payments) for stays that ended within the last 90 days, are in-house or are still to come. |
| **Older than 90d** | Money still owed by guests who left more than 90 days ago. |
| **In-house receivables** | Money owed by guests staying tonight. |

Below:
- **By source this month:** this month's revenue split by where it came from, with a share bar.
- **Outstanding balances:** every guest who owes money, biggest first, with how much of the total they have paid.

**To include very old unpaid stays in the list**
1. Open the **Revenue & collection** tab.
2. Tick **Include older** above the **Outstanding balances** list.

**Good to know**
- Ticking **Include older** also changes the **Guest receivables** card on the **Cash control** tab.

#### Tab: Tasks & issues
- Cards: **Overdue** (past the target date and not complete), **Due today**, **Pending**, **Complete**.
- Lists of the overdue tasks and the tasks due today, with department, project, the responsible person and how many days late.
- Tap a task to open the Tasks screen.

#### Tab: Cash control
- Cards: **Cash on hand** (all active accounts added up), **No invoice / receipt** (expenses with no file attached), **Guest receivables** (money guests owe), **Revenue this month**.
- **Accounts:** one card per account with balance and last movement.
- **Latest expenses:** the last 10 expenses by payment date. A **No invoice** label marks those without a file. An expense paid in another currency also shows its original amount.

### Partner dashboard

**What it's for:** A read-only summary for owners and partners: money in and out, who has paid in what, budget against reality, how full the rooms are, and what is coming next.

**Where to find it:** On a computer, look in the left sidebar under **Dashboards** and click **Partner dashboard**. On a phone, tap **More** in the bottom bar, then **Partner dashboard**.

#### What you see

Under the title you see the property name, the number of partners and today's date. Below that is a row of eight tabs. On a phone, swipe the row sideways to reach the later tabs.

| Tab | What it answers |
|---|---|
| **Financial overview** | How much came in, how much went out, and what is in the accounts |
| **Funding & investment** | How much each partner has paid in against what they agreed |
| **Shareholding** | Each partner's ownership share against what they have paid |
| **Budget vs actual** | Plan against reality for one year |
| **Business KPIs** | How full the rooms were and what a night earned |
| **Cost structure** | Where the money is spent, by category |
| **Forecast** | Expected cash and the bookings still to arrive |
| **Partners** | A profile card for each partner |

All amounts are shown in your company's main currency.

#### Financial overview tab

| Figure | Meaning and how it is worked out |
|---|---|
| **Total revenue** | Every revenue record ever entered, added together ("all time"). |
| **Total expenses** | Every expense ever entered, added together ("all time"). |
| **Net position** | Total revenue plus all capital paid in by partners, minus total expenses. |
| **Account total** | The balances of all active accounts added together. The card also shows how many accounts there are. |

Below the cards:

- **Revenue by source**: revenue grouped into **Direct Booking**, **Booking.com**, **Agoda**, **Airbnb** and **Other**, largest first, each with its amount and its share of the total.
- **Accounts**: each active account with its type, its balance, and a **Total** line.
- **Monthly revenue vs expenses**: two bars for each month of the chosen year (**Revenue** and **Expenses**), plus a one-line summary of the year's revenue, expenses and the difference. Revenue is placed in the month of its date; expenses in the month of their payment date.

#### Funding & investment tab

| Figure | Meaning and how it is worked out |
|---|---|
| **Total capital** | All contributions recorded on the **Capital** screen, added together. The card also shows how many contributions there are. |
| **Partners** | The number of partners shown on the dashboard. |
| **Agreed capital** | The **Agreed capital** amounts from all partner profiles, added together. |
| **Funded** | Total capital divided by agreed capital, as a percentage. Shows a dash if no agreed capital has been entered. |

Below is one card per partner with their ownership percentage, **Contributed** (what they paid in, and the agreed amount after the slash), and a bar showing how far along they are, for example "75% of agreed". The bar stops at 100%. If the profile has no agreed amount, it says "No agreed capital set".

If some capital was recorded under a name that does not match any partner profile, a note at the bottom tells you how much, and asks you to link it on the **Capital** screen.

#### Shareholding tab

| Figure | Meaning and how it is worked out |
|---|---|
| **Total allocated** | All partners' ownership percentages added together. It should be 100%. The card says "ownership adds up" or "should be 100%". |
| **Fully settled** | How many partners have paid at least their share (their **Balance** is zero or below). |
| **Still owed** | The balances of all partners who still owe money, added together. |
| **Total capital** | All capital paid in so far. This is the base for the **Nominal** amounts. |

The table has one row per partner:

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Partner** | The partner's name. |
| **Ownership** | Their agreed share of the company, in percent. |
| **Nominal** | What their share is worth: total capital multiplied by their ownership percentage. |
| **Paid in** | What they have actually contributed. |
| **Balance** | Nominal minus paid in. A positive number means the partner still owes; a negative number means they paid more than their share. |
| **Funded** | Paid in divided by nominal, as a percentage. |

#### Budget vs actual tab

This tab compares the budget for one year with what really happened in that year.

| Figure | Meaning and how it is worked out |
|---|---|
| **Revenue** | The big number is the revenue dated in the year. The small line shows the budget. |
| **Expenses** | The big number is the expenses paid in the year. The small line shows the budget. |
| **Net result** | Actual revenue minus actual expenses. The small line shows the budgeted result. |
| **Variance** | The actual net result minus the budgeted net result. Positive means better than planned. |

Three tables follow, each with the columns **Item**, **Budget**, **Actual** and **Variance**, and a **Total** line:

- **Revenue**: a single line, **Room revenue**.
- **Operating expenses (OPEX)**: day-to-day running costs, one line per expense category. Expenses with no category appear as **Uncategorised**.
- **Capital expenditure (CAPEX)**: larger one-off purchases. Each category is followed by its budgeted items. The items show a budget only; their **Actual** is a dash.

**Variance** always means actual minus budget, with the percentage of the budget in brackets. Green means good news (more revenue than planned, or less spending than planned). Red means the opposite.

The box at the bottom shows **Net budget**, **Net actual** and **Variance**. Net means revenue minus OPEX minus CAPEX.

#### Business KPIs tab

These figures are for one month, worked out from the bookings of the property you are currently working in. The line at the top shows how many active rooms and how many days were used.

| Figure | Meaning and how it is worked out |
|---|---|
| **Occupancy** | The share of available room nights that were sold. Nights booked in the month, divided by (active rooms × days in the month). |
| **ADR** | Average price per sold night. The month's booking revenue divided by the nights sold. |
| **RevPAR** | Revenue per available room night, whether sold or not. The month's booking revenue divided by (active rooms × days in the month). |
| **Avg stay** | Nights sold in the month divided by the number of bookings that touch the month. Shown as nights, for example "3.5 n". |

The month's booking revenue is each booking's stay total spread evenly over its nights; only the nights that fall inside the month are counted. So a 10-night stay with 4 nights in this month counts for four tenths of its total.

Below, **Last 12 months** lists the same figures month by month, newest first: **Month**, **Occupancy**, **ADR**, **RevPAR**, **Nights**, **Bookings** and **Revenue**. Occupancy is green at 70% or more, amber from 40%, red below that.

#### Cost structure tab

| Figure | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Total expenses** | All expenses ever entered, added together. |
| **Categories** | How many different expense categories have been used. |
| **Largest** | The category with the most spending, and its amount. |

- Revenue, expenses, capital and accounts cover the whole company. The KPIs, the forecast bookings and the budgeted revenue cover only the property you are currently working in.

- If the property has no active rooms, the KPIs are worked out as if it had one room.
- On a phone, tables show as cards and the ownership form slides up from the bottom. On a computer, they are tables and the form opens as a pop-up in the middle.

## Part 2: Operations

### Guests

**What it's for:** Recording every booking: who stays in which room, for how long, at what price, and what they have paid. This is the most important screen in the app. Revenue, the room calendar and guest invoices all come from here.

**Where to find it:** Computer: left sidebar, **Operations**, **Guests**. Phone: the **Guests** tab in the bottom bar.

#### What you see

**The four cards at the top**

| Card | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **In-house** | Bookings staying tonight. Tap to show only these. |
| **Arriving 7d** | Bookings that start within the next 7 days. Tap to show all upcoming bookings. |
| **Departing 7d** | Guests who are in-house now and leave within the next 7 days. |
| **Outstanding** | All money guests still owe, added up. |

The "7" follows the number of days set in Settings.

**The list**
Each booking shows the room, guest name, dates, nights, **Total**, **Paid** and **Balance** (total minus paid; red when money is still owed). Small labels tell you more:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Upcoming** | Check-in is in the future. |
| **In-House** | The guest is staying now. |
| **Checked Out** | The check-out date is today or earlier. |
| **TM30 filed / TM30 pending** | Whether the immigration notification box is ticked on the booking. |
| **Revenue** | The booking is counted as revenue. |
| **Legal** | The booking is shown on the Legal & accounting screen. |
| A paperclip | A document is attached. |

Under the list is a totals line for the bookings currently shown: **Total**, **Paid** and **Balance**.

#### Add a booking
1. Open **Guests**.
2. Tap **Add booking** (top right). The **New booking** form opens.
3. Under **Stay**: choose the **Room**, type the **Guest name**, set **Check-in** and **Check-out**, choose the **Booking source** (for example Direct Booking or Booking.com) and type the number of **Guests**.
4. Under **Pricing**: type the **Rate per night**. **Total stay** fills in by itself as rate times nights. If you agreed a different total, type it into **Total stay**.
5. Under **Payment**: choose the **Payment method** and type the **Amount paid** so far. Leave both empty if nothing was paid yet.
6. Under **Contact & immigration**: fill in **Email**, **Phone**, **Passport number** and **Passport expiry** if you have them. Tick **TM30 immigration notification filed** once you have registered the guest with immigration.
7. Under **Reporting**: leave **Add to revenue** ticked if this booking is income. Tick **Show on legal page** if your lawyer or accountant should see it.
8. Under **Notes & attachment**: type any **Notes** and attach a file under **Attached document** (for example a passport photo or booking confirmation) if you wish.
9. Check the **Balance** shown at the bottom of the form, then tap **Save booking**.

**Good to know**
- The top of the form shows the number of nights. The check-out day is not counted as a night. If check-in and check-out are the same day it says "Same-day stay".
- Check-out cannot be before check-in.
- Only active rooms can be chosen.
- After typing your own **Total stay**, a small link **Use rate × nights** appears under the field. Tap it to go back to the automatic total.
- The **Payment method** decides which account the money is added to (for example Cash goes to the Cash account). If you leave it as **Not set**, no account changes.
- This form does not close when you tap outside it, so you cannot lose a half-filled booking by accident. Use **Cancel** or the X to close it.
- The app does not stop you from putting two bookings in the same room on the same dates. Check the [Occupancy](#occupancy) calendar if you are unsure.

#### Record payments in several parts
Use this when a guest pays a deposit and then the rest later.

1. Open the booking with the pencil icon (**Edit**).
2. Under **Payment**, fill in **Payment 1 date** and **Payment 1 amount**.
3. When the next payment arrives, fill in **Payment 2 date** and **Payment 2 amount**. There is room for three payments.
4. Tap **Save booking**.

**Good to know**
- As soon as one of the dated payments has an amount, the **Amount paid** field is locked and shows the sum of the dated payments.
- All payments of one booking go to the one **Payment method** chosen on the booking.

#### A guest checks in or checks out
There is no check-in or check-out button. The app works out the status from the dates:
- On the check-in date the booking turns to **In-House** by itself.
- On the check-out date it turns to **Checked Out** by itself.

What you do at the desk:
1. Open the booking with the pencil icon (**Edit**).
2. At check-in: add the passport details and tick **TM30 immigration notification filed** when it is done.
3. At check-out: record the final payment so the **Balance** becomes zero.
4. Tap **Save booking**.

If the guest arrives or leaves on a different day than planned, change **Check-in** or **Check-out** on the booking.

#### Edit or delete a booking
1. Find the booking (search by guest, room, email or passport).
2. Tap the pencil icon (**Edit**), change what you need and tap **Save booking**.
3. To delete, tap the bin icon (**Delete**) and confirm.

**Good to know**
- Changing the total, the dates or the source also updates the booking's line on the Revenue screen.
- Deleting a booking also removes its revenue line and the payments it added to your accounts. Invoices already created are kept for the record.

#### Find and filter bookings
1. Type in the search box: guest name, room, email, phone, passport number or booking source.
2. Tap **All**, **Upcoming**, **In-house** or **Checked out** to filter by status.
3. On a computer, click a column heading to sort. The list starts with the latest check-in first.

#### Create an invoice for a guest
1. Find the booking.
2. Tap the document icon (**Create invoice**).
3. The invoice opens in a new browser tab.
4. Tap **Print / Save PDF** at the top of the invoice to print it or save it as a PDF.

**What is on the invoice**
- Your company details, tax ID, address and signature name (set in [Settings](#settings) under invoices).
- The invoice number, for example INV-2026-0001. Numbers run in order and start again each year.
- **Bill to:** the guest's name, email, phone and passport number.
- One line for the room charge with the number of nights and the price per night.
- **Subtotal**, **VAT**, **Total**, **Deposit / paid** and **Balance due**. **Balance due** is what the guest still has to pay. When it is zero the invoice says "Paid in full".

**Good to know**
- Once an invoice exists, the icon's label changes to its number (for example **Invoice INV-2026-0001**) and tapping it opens the same invoice again.
- The invoice is a copy of the booking at the moment it was first created. If you change the price or record another payment afterwards, the invoice does not change, and there is currently no button to make a fresh one. So record all payments on the booking before you create the invoice.
- The invoice date is the check-out date, or today if the guest has not checked out yet.
- The invoice link works without signing in. Anyone who has the link can open the invoice, so only send it to the guest.
- Creating an invoice needs permission to add bookings. If your role may only view, you will see an error message.

#### Email an invoice to a guest
1. Find the booking.
2. Tap the envelope icon (**Email invoice**).
3. Your own email app opens with a new message already written: the guest's email address, the subject "Invoice (number)" and a short text with the invoice link.
4. Check the message and press send in your email app.

**Good to know**
- Sojourn does not send emails by itself. It only prepares the message in your email app. If no email app is set up on your device, nothing will open. In that case create the invoice, copy the address from the browser tab and paste it into any message.
- If the booking has no email address, the "To" line is empty and you type it yourself.
- If the booking has no invoice yet, one is created first.

#### Open a booking's attachment
Tap the open icon (**Open attachment**) on the booking. It is only shown when a file is attached.

#### Export the guest list
Tap **Export** and choose a format. The file includes room, guest, dates, nights, totals, payments, contact details, passport number, TM30 status and notes. Treat it as confidential.

### Occupancy

**What it's for:** Seeing at a glance which rooms are taken on which nights, and how full you are in any month.

**Where to find it:** Computer: left sidebar, **Operations**, **Occupancy**. Phone: **More**, then **Occupancy**.

#### What you see

**The six cards**

| Card | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Occupancy** | The share of your room nights that are sold, as a percentage. Example: 10 rooms in a 30-day month gives 300 room nights. If 150 are booked, occupancy is 50%. |
| **Nights sold** | The number of booked room nights in this month. |
| **Bookings** | The number of stays that touch this month. |
| **Room revenue** | The money from stays in this month. If a stay runs across two months, only the nights inside this month are counted, at the stay's average price per night. |

| **RevPAR** | Revenue per available room night: room revenue divided by all room nights, sold or empty. It shows how well you fill the whole property, not only the rooms you sold. |

**The calendar**
- One row per active room, one column per day. Today is highlighted.
- A coloured bar shows a stay. The guest's first name is written at the start of the bar. Each guest has their own colour.
- The bar ends the night before check-out, because the check-out day is not a night stayed.
- If two bookings overlap in one room, the bars are drawn one above the other, so a double booking is easy to spot.

**Guests this month**
A list of the guest names with their colours.

#### See how full you are in another month
1. Tap the right arrow (**Next month**) or the left arrow (**Previous month**).
2. Or tap the month box between the arrows and pick any month and year.
3. Tap **This month** to return to the current month. The button only shows when you are looking at another month.

#### Look at one booking
1. Tap a coloured bar, or tap a name under **Guests this month**.
2. A pop-up shows check-in, check-out, nights in this month, **Stay total**, **Paid**, **Balance**, **Source**, number of guests and TM30 status.
3. Tap **Open guests** to go to the Guests screen, or **Close**.

**Good to know**
- You cannot add or change bookings here. Do that on [Guests](#guests).
- The figures come from the bookings (their **Total stay**), not from payments received.
- Only active rooms count. If a booking sits in a room that was switched off or deleted, a note under the calendar says it is "counted in the figures but not drawn".
- On a phone the calendar is wide. Swipe sideways to see the whole month.
- The Occupancy card turns green at 70% or more and amber at 40% or more.

| **Filtered total** | The total of the expenses currently shown, and how many they are. |
| **Legal expenses** | The total of expenses shared with legal. Tap to show only these. |
| **Records** | How many expenses there are. |

**The list**
Each expense shows the date, **Transaction #**, **Doc #**, category, supplier and description, **Amount**, payment method, who paid, and two labels:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Audit** | Your own bookkeeping check: **To check** (new), **Checked**, or **YES**. |
| **Legal** | Only on expenses shared with legal. Shows the reviewer's decision: **Pending**, **Accepted**, **Clarification Needed** or **Rejected**. |

A paperclip means a receipt or invoice is attached.

#### Add an expense by hand
1. Tap **Add expense**. The **New expense** form opens.
2. Under **Document**: set the **Payment date**. Leave **Transaction #** and **Document #** empty and the app numbers them for you. Choose a **Category**. **Subcategory** and **Type** (for example OPEX or CAPEX) are optional.
3. Under **Amount & payment**: type the **Supplier** and the **Amount**. Change the **Currency** if you paid in another currency.
4. Choose the **Payment method**. The amount is taken from the account linked to that method.
5. In **Paid by**, type or choose who paid (a partner, a team member or "Other").
6. Leave **Audit** as **To check** unless you have already checked it.
7. Under **Description & legal**: type a **Description**. Tick **Share with legal** if the lawyer or accountant should review this expense.
8. Under **Attachment**: tap **Choose file** (or **Camera** on a phone) to attach the **Receipt or invoice**.
9. Tap **Save expense**.

**Good to know**
- Automatic numbers look like "Expense 001", "Expense 002" for the transaction and "001", "002" for the document. The message after saving tells you the number that was given.
- The form does not close when you tap outside it. Use **Cancel** or the X.

#### Record an expense from a receipt photo (Scan receipt)
1. Tap **Add expense**.
2. At the top of the form, tap **Scan receipt** and choose a photo of the receipt. On a phone you can tap **Camera** and take the photo now.
3. Wait a few seconds. The app reads the receipt and fills in what it can: **Supplier**, **Document #**, **Payment date**, **Amount**, **Currency**, **Description** and **Category**. The fields it filled in are outlined for a few seconds, and a message says how many fields were filled.
4. Check every filled field against the receipt. Correct anything that is wrong.
5. Tap **Attach this image** next to the small picture of the receipt. The label changes to **Attached**.
6. Choose the **Payment method** and fill in anything still missing.
7. Tap **Save expense**.

**Good to know**
- Scanning only reads the receipt. The photo is not kept unless you tap **Attach this image**. If you forget, the expense is saved without a receipt and counts in the "No invoice / receipt" total.
- Scanning only fills empty fields. It never overwrites something you already typed.
- The category is only filled in when the app is confident it matches one of your own categories.
- Scanning works with photos and pictures, not with PDF files. Attach a PDF with **Choose file** instead and type the details.
- The reading is done by an automatic service and can make mistakes, especially with handwriting or faded paper. Always check the amount and date.
- To throw away a scan, tap the X next to the picture (**Discard scan**). The fields that were filled stay as they are.
- You can scan at most 60 receipts per hour.
- Scanning has to be switched on by whoever runs the service. If it is not, you see a message that receipt scanning "is not switched on for this service yet". You can still add the expense by hand and attach the photo.

#### An expense in another currency
1. In the form, choose the **Currency** the receipt is in.
2. Type the **Amount** in that currency.
3. A note under the amount shows roughly what it is worth in your base currency.
4. Save.

**Good to know**
- The app works out the base-currency value once, when you save, with the exchange rate it has at that moment.
- In the list, the expense shows both amounts, for example "€120 ≈ ฿4,440".
- If the app has no exchange rate for that currency yet, the expense is saved but shows "no rate", and it is left out of totals. The line under the screen title then says for example "2 without an exchange rate". Refresh the rates (tap the dot next to the currency switch), then open the expense and save it again.

#### Edit or delete an expense
1. Tap the pencil icon (**Edit**), change what you need and tap **Save expense**.
2. Or tap the bin icon (**Delete**) and confirm.

**Good to know**
- Deleting an expense also puts the money back in the account it was paid from.

#### Share an expense with the lawyer or accountant
1. Edit the expense.
2. Tick **Share with legal**.
3. Tap **Save expense**.

The expense now appears on the [Legal & accounting](#legal--accounting) screen with the status **Pending**, waiting for review. The reviewer's decision shows back here in the **Legal** column.

#### Find and filter expenses
1. Search by supplier, description, transaction number or document number.
2. Choose a category in the **All categories** box.
3. Tap **Legal only** to see only expenses shared with legal.
4. Tap a year button, or **All years**.

#### Find expenses with no receipt
The **No invoice / receipt** card on the Overview and Operations screens tells you how many expenses have no file. On the Expenses screen, the ones without a paperclip are the ones to fix: edit each one and attach the receipt.

#### Export expenses
Tap **Export**. The file includes both the original amount with its currency and the **Amount (base)**, plus the audit and legal status.

### Accounts

**What it's for:** See how much money sits in each bank account, cash box and e-wallet, and the list of money going in and out.

**Where to find it:** On a computer, look in the left sidebar under **Finance** and click **Accounts**. On a phone, tap **More** in the bottom bar, then **Accounts**.

#### What you see

Four number cards at the top:

2. Change what you need.
3. Tap **Save account**.

Use this when the real balance differs from the app, for example after a bank fee or a cash count.
1. On the account's card, tap **Adjust balance**.
2. Under **Direction**, choose **Add money (+)** or **Remove money (−)**.
3. Type the **Amount**.

5. Type a **Description**, for example "Bank fee". If you leave it empty the app writes "Balance adjustment".

3. Tap **Delete** to confirm.

1. To see one account, choose it in the **All accounts** list.

1. Set the filters and search so the list shows what you want.
2. Tap **Export**.

**Good to know**
- Balances move by themselves. When a booking payment is recorded with a payment method that is linked to an account, the money is added to that account. When an expense is paid with a linked payment method, the money is taken out. The link is set in **Settings**, **Payments**.
- If a payment method is not linked to an account, nothing is added to or taken from any balance.
- Lines marked **auto** cannot be deleted here. Change or delete the booking or the expense, and the line follows.
- Deleting an account also deletes all its movements, and payment methods linked to it stop updating balances. If you only want to stop using an account, untick **Active** instead.
- Inactive accounts are left out of **Total cash on hand**.
- Changing the **Opening balance** of an existing account shifts its balance. For corrections, use **Adjust balance** so there is a dated line explaining the change.
- All movements are recorded in your main currency, even if the account is set to another currency. In that case the card shows a small currency tag.
- The movements list shows the latest 500 lines. Account balances always count every movement, including older ones.
- **Inflows** and **Outflows** follow the account and date filters, but not the search box. The **Net** line under the list follows the search box too.
- **Adjust balance** is shown to people allowed to add in Accounts. Buttons you are not allowed to use are hidden.
- On a phone, movements show as cards, forms slide up from the bottom, and there are no column headers to sort by. On a computer, movements are a table and forms open as a pop-up in the middle.

### Budget

**What it's for:** Plan the income and costs you expect for a year, then compare the plan with what really happened.

**Where to find it:** On a computer, look in the left sidebar under **Finance** and click **Budget**. On a phone, tap **More** in the bottom bar, then **Budget**.

#### What you see

The top line shows the property name and the year. On the right is the **Year** list. Below are three tabs:

| Tab | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Setup** | Reference room prices and target occupancy for each room. The small number (for example 3/8) says how many rooms are filled in. |
| **Input** | Where you type the plan: **Revenue matrix**, **Expense items** and **Rent schedule**. This tab opens first. |
| **Control** | Plan compared with real figures. Nothing is typed here. |

Words used on this screen:
- **Budget**: the amount you planned.
- **Actual**: the amount really recorded in the app.
- **Variance**: actual minus budget. Green is good news, red is bad news.
- **OPEX**: day-to-day running costs (salaries, electricity, cleaning).
- **CAPEX**: one-off purchases and building work (furniture, renovation).
- **P&L**: a summary of income, costs and what is left.
- **Result** or **Net result**: income minus costs.

The budget belongs to the property you are working in. If you have several properties, each has its own budget.

#### Change the year
1. Open the **Year** list (top right).
2. Pick a year. Last year, this year and next year are always offered, plus any year that already has figures.

The **Setup**, **Revenue matrix**, **Expense items** and **Control** figures change with the year. The **Rent schedule** does not.

#### Setup tab: fill in room prices and targets
1. Tap the **Setup** tab.
2. Tap **Create rows for all rooms** to prepare an empty line for every room that has none. The number in brackets says how many are missing. (Optional.)
3. On a room's line, tap the plus icon (**Set up**) if it is tagged **Not set**, or the pencil icon (**Edit**) if it already has figures.
4. Fill in **High season rate** and **Low season rate** (price per night), **Target occupancy %** (0 to 100) and **Notes**.
5. Tap **Save**.

To remove a room's figures, tap the bin icon (**Delete**), then **Delete**.

These figures are for your reference only. They do not fill in the revenue matrix and are not used in **Control**.

#### Input tab: plan revenue (Revenue matrix)
The matrix is a grid with one line per active room and one column per month (Jan to Dec), plus a **Total** column and a **Total** line.
1. Tap the **Input** tab.
2. Under **Revenue matrix**, tap the cell where the room and the month meet. Empty cells show a dash.
3. Type the **Amount** you expect that room to earn in that month.
4. Type a **Season** if you want (**High**, **Low** and **Shoulder** are suggested). It is optional.
5. Tap **Save**.

To change a cell, tap it again, change the amount, and tap **Save**.

To empty a cell, tap it, tap **Clear**, then **Clear** again to confirm.

#### Input tab: add an expense item
1. Under **Expense items**, tap **Add item**. (Once items exist, you can also tap **Add OPEX item** or **Add CAPEX item** inside the grid.)
2. Type the **Item** name, for example "Electricity".
3. Choose the **Category**. The list is the same as on the expense form.
4. Choose the **Type**: **OPEX** or **CAPEX**.
5. Type an **Amount** if you already know it. You can leave it empty and fill the months later.
6. In **Apply to**, choose **All 12 months** or one month. This list only works once an amount is typed.
7. Tap **Add item**.

The grid shows a band **OPEX · operating** and a band **CAPEX · capital**. Inside each band, items are grouped by category. Category lines are in bold and add up their items. Below are **Total OPEX**, **Total CAPEX** and **Total expenses**.

#### Input tab: change an expense amount for one month
1. Tap the cell where the item and the month meet.
2. Type the **Amount**.
3. Type a **Subcategory** if you want. It is optional.
4. Tap **Save**.

#### Input tab: use the same amount for every month
1. Tap any month cell of the item.
2. Type the **Amount**.
3. Tap **Copy this amount to all 12 months**.
4. Tap **Copy** to confirm. Amounts already typed in other months are replaced.

#### Input tab: delete an expense amount
1. Tap the cell.
2. Tap **Delete**, then **Delete** again to confirm.

Only that month is deleted. An item disappears from the grid once all its months are deleted.

#### Input tab: Rent schedule
Use this to note the yearly rent for each year of your lease.
1. Under **Rent schedule**, tap **Add year**.
2. Check the **Year number** (1 for the first lease year, 2 for the second, and so on). The next number is filled in for you.
3. Type a **Label** if you want, for example "2026–2027".
4. Type the **Annual rent**.
5. Check the **VAT**. The app fills it in from the rent and your VAT rate. You can type another amount.
6. The form shows the **Monthly** amount (annual rent divided by 12).
7. Tap **Save**.

To change a year, tap the pencil icon (**Edit**). To remove it, tap the bin icon (**Delete**), then **Delete**.

The list shows **Year**, **Label**, **Annual rent**, **VAT** and **Monthly**, with total rent and total VAT underneath.

#### Control tab: compare plan and reality
1. Tap the **Control** tab.
2. Read the six cards: **Budget revenue**, **Actual revenue**, **Budget expenses**, **Actual expenses**, **Budget result**, **Actual result**. Under each "Actual" card is the variance, as an amount and a percentage.
3. Tap **P&L** to see one table for the whole year, with the columns **Line**, **Budget**, **Actual** and **Variance**. It lists **Revenue**, each OPEX category, **Total OPEX**, each CAPEX item, **Total CAPEX**, **Total expenses** and **Net result**.
4. Tap **Monthly** to see the same comparison month by month, for **Revenue**, **Expenses** and **Result**, with a **Total** line.
5. If you have more than one property, use the list that reads **Actuals: all properties** to switch to the current property only.

How to read the colours: for revenue and result, more than planned is green. For expenses, more than planned is red.

#### Export
1. Go to the part you want: the **Setup** list, the **Revenue matrix**, **Expense items**, the **Rent schedule**, or the **P&L** or **Monthly** view in **Control**.
2. Tap **Export** next to it.
3. Choose **Excel (.xlsx)**, **CSV**, **JSON** or **Print / PDF**.

Each part exports separately. There is no single export of the whole budget.

**Good to know**
- "Actual" revenue comes from the **Revenue** screen, by date. "Actual" expenses come from the **Expenses** screen, by payment date. You cannot type actual figures in the budget.
- The plan is for one property, but **Control** compares it with the real figures of all your properties unless you switch the list to the current property.
- Real expenses are matched to budget lines by category name. Use the same category for the budget item and for the real expenses, or they will show on separate lines.
- A category with real expenses but no budget line still appears in the P&L, with a budget of zero.
- The app does not know whether a real expense is OPEX or CAPEX. All real expenses are counted under OPEX. CAPEX lines show a dash in the **Actual** column.
- The **Rent schedule** is a reference list. It is not tied to the year you selected and is not added to **Expense items** or **Control**. To include rent in the comparison, also add it as an expense item.
- Saving a cell with the amount left empty stores zero. Use **Clear** (revenue) or **Delete** (expenses) to really empty it.
- An item's name, category and type cannot be changed after it is added. Delete its months and add it again.
- An item added without an amount is created with zero in January so that its line shows in the grid.
- The same item name cannot be added twice in the same category and type for the same year.
- A cell with a dotted underline holds more than one entry. **Save** changes the first entry. For revenue, **Clear** removes them all. For expenses, **Delete** removes one at a time.
- Only active rooms have a line in the revenue matrix. If budget figures exist for a room that was renamed or switched off, a warning above the grid says they are not counted. In **Setup**, such a line is tagged **Not an active room**.
- If the property has no active rooms, the matrix is empty and shows a **Manage rooms** button.
- If a year has more than 5,000 revenue lines or 5,000 expense lines, **Control** warns that totals may be incomplete.
- The percentage is not calculated when the budget for a line is zero.
- Cells can only be tapped by people whose role allows adding or editing in Budget. Buttons you are not allowed to use are hidden.
- The grids are wide. On a phone, swipe sideways to see all months. The first column stays in place. The **Setup** and **Rent schedule** lists show as cards on a phone and as tables on a computer, where you can click a column header to sort. Forms slide up from the bottom on a phone and open as a pop-up in the middle on a computer.

#### If the vault is locked

The vault only works after whoever runs the app for you has set a secret key on the system. If that has not been done, a warning box appears at the top of the screen:

1. **Add log-in**, **Reveal** and the pencil icon are greyed out. Hovering over the pencil shows "Encryption key missing".

- You can save a log-in without a password. **Reveal** then answers "No password is stored for this log-in yet. Edit it to set one."

- If copying fails, you see "Clipboard is not available in this browser". Show the password and type it by hand instead.

2. Read the top box: your plan, the days left in a free trial, and the price for your number of people.

**Good to know**

3. Set the **Prices include VAT** switch. On: your room rates already contain VAT and the invoice shows the VAT part separately. Off: VAT is added on top of the room rate on the invoice.
4. Tap **Save changes**.

**To change alert timings**
1. Open **Settings**, then **Organisation**.
2. Under **Alerts**, set **Fire extinguisher warning (days before expiry)**, **Arrivals window (days)** and **Departures window (days)**.
3. Tap **Save changes**.

**To set the default partner hourly rate**
1. Open **Settings**, then **Organisation**.
2. Under **Partners**, type the amount in **Partner hour rate**.
3. Tap **Save changes**.

**To add a shortcut link**
1. Open **Settings**, then **Organisation**.
2. Under **External links**, tap **Add link**.
3. Type a name in the first box (for example "Immigration portal") and the full web address, starting with https://, in the second.
4. Tap **Save changes**.

To remove a link, tap the bin icon (**Remove link**) next to it, then **Save changes**.

**Good to know**
- **Base currency** is the currency every amount is saved in. Changing it does **not** convert the amounts you already entered. The numbers stay the same and are simply labelled with the new currency. The app shows a warning when you pick a different one. Only change it on a brand-new organisation.
- Display currencies are for viewing only. Once you have at least one, a small currency switch appears (top of the screen on a phone, bottom of the sidebar on a computer) and each person can flip between the base currency and the display currencies. With no display currencies the switch is hidden.
- Amounts are converted with the daily European Central Bank rate. The small dot on the currency switch is green when rates are fresh and amber when they are missing or more than a week old. Tap the dot to fetch new rates.
- You can choose up to 8 display currencies, picked from the list shown. You cannot type in a currency that is not listed.
- Each person's chosen display currency is remembered in their own browser.
- A new organisation starts with VAT at 7% and **Prices include VAT** switched on.
- A change to VAT applies to invoices created from then on. Invoices already created keep the VAT they were made with.
- **Fiscal year starts in** is saved, and the form says it is used by budgets and year-to-date figures. At the time of writing, changing it does not change any figures in the app.
- The fire extinguisher warning (0 to 365 days) controls how early Operations and the room pages warn you about an expiry date. The arrivals and departures windows (0 to 90 days) set how many days ahead count as "arriving soon" and "departing soon" on Overview and Guests.
- **Partner hour rate** is the rate used for partner work hours when nothing else is set. A partner's own profile can have a different rate.
- Links appear as shortcut buttons on the Guests and Operations screens. You can have up to 20.

## Roles and who can see what

Every person in your organisation has one role. The role decides which screens they see in the menu and what they are allowed to do on each one: **View** (look), **Add** (create new records), **Edit** (change existing records) and **Delete** (remove records).

A new organisation starts with seven built-in roles. The person who creates the organisation is the Owner.

### The seven built-in roles

| Role | Meant for | Can see | Can change | Cannot see |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Owner** | The owner or main administrator | Everything | Everything, including Settings, the team and roles | Nothing is hidden |
| **Manager** | General manager running the business day to day | Everything | Everything except Settings, which is look-only | Nothing is hidden, but Settings cannot be changed |
| **Finance** | Accountant or bookkeeper | Everything except the two screens on the right | Full control of Overview, Revenue, Expenses, Accounts, Legal & accounting, Budget and Capital. The rest is look-only | Log-in vault, Settings |
| **Legal** | Outside lawyer or accounting firm | Revenue, Expenses, Legal & accounting, Documents | Can add and edit on Legal & accounting only (no deleting). The rest is look-only | All other screens |
| **Partner** | Co-owner or shareholder | Everything except the two screens on the right | Can add and edit (no deleting) on Partner dashboard, Tasks, Budget, Partners, Capital, Partner work and Meetings. The rest is look-only | Log-in vault, Settings |
| **Staff** | Reception, housekeeping and other daily staff | Operations, Guests, Occupancy, Rooms, Tasks, Complaints | Can add and edit on those six screens (no deleting) | All money, partner and company screens, Overview, Staff hours, Settings |
| **Viewer** | Someone who only needs to look | Everything except the two screens on the right | Nothing | Log-in vault, Settings |

### Screen by screen

"Full" means View, Add, Edit and Delete. "Add/edit" means View, Add and Edit but no Delete. "View" means look only. "No" means the screen is hidden.

| Screen | Owner | Manager | Finance | Legal | Partner | Staff | Viewer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Overview | Full | Full | Full | No | View | No | View |
| Operations | Full | Full | View | No | View | Add/edit | View |
| Partner dashboard | Full | Full | View | No | Add/edit | No | View |

### Good to know

- **These are the starting values.** Someone who is allowed to edit Settings can change what any role may do, rename it, delete it or create new roles under **Settings**, then **Roles**. Only the **Owner** role is locked: it always has everything and cannot be edited, renamed or deleted. If a role in your organisation behaves differently from the table, it has been changed there.
- **A missing menu item means your role does not allow it.** The menu only lists screens your role can view. Nothing is broken. Ask an Owner if you need access.
- **Missing buttons work the same way.** If you can open a screen but see no button to add, edit or delete, your role is look-only (or has no Delete) on that screen.
- **Opening a screen you are not allowed to see** (for example from an old bookmark) does not show an error. The app quietly takes you to the first screen your role can view. If your role can view no screen at all, you land on your own profile.
- **Everyone can open their own profile**, whatever their role, to change their name and password: the person icon at the bottom of the sidebar on a computer, or **More** then **Profile** on a phone.
- **"View" on Settings is not the same as running Settings.** A Manager can read every settings page and download data, but cannot save changes, invite people or change roles. That needs **Edit** on Settings. Wiping data with **Reset data** needs **Delete** on Settings.
- **Role changes apply to everyone with that role** as soon as they are saved. The person does not need to sign out.
- **One role per person per organisation.** If you belong to several organisations you can have a different role in each, for example Owner of your own and Viewer in a partner's. **Settings**, then **Profile**, lists your organisations and your role in each.
- **There must always be at least one active Owner.** The only Owner cannot be given another role, suspended or removed until someone else is made an Owner.
- **On a phone**, the bottom bar shows up to four screens your role can open, plus **More** for the rest.

## Common tasks cheat-sheet

Short recipes for everyday jobs. Each one points to the full instructions above.

### 1. Take a new booking
1. Open **Guests** and tap **Add booking**.
2. Choose the **Room**, type the **Guest name**, set **Check-in** and **Check-out**.
3. Type the **Rate per night** (or the **Total stay**).
4. If a deposit was paid, choose the **Payment method** and fill in **Payment 1 date** and **Payment 1 amount**.
5. Tap **Save booking**.

### 2. A guest checks in
1. Open **Guests**, find the booking and tap the pencil icon (**Edit**).
2. Add **Passport number** and **Passport expiry**.
3. Tick **TM30 immigration notification filed** once the guest is registered.
4. Tap **Save booking**. The status changes to **In-House** by itself on the check-in date.

### 3. A guest checks out and pays
1. Open **Guests**, find the booking and tap the pencil icon (**Edit**).
2. Record the final payment (next free **Payment date** and **Payment amount**, or **Amount paid**) so the **Balance** at the bottom is zero.
3. Tap **Save booking**.
4. Tap the document icon (**Create invoice**), then **Print / Save PDF**.

### 4. Send a guest their invoice
1. Make sure all payments are recorded on the booking first.
2. Open **Guests** and find the booking.
3. Tap the envelope icon (**Email invoice**).
4. Your own email app opens with the message and the invoice link ready. Press send there.

### 5. Record an expense from a receipt photo
1. Open **Expenses** and tap **Add expense**.
2. Tap **Scan receipt** (or **Camera** on a phone) and pick or take the photo.
3. Check the filled-in fields.
4. Tap **Attach this image**.
5. Choose the **Payment method** and tap **Save expense**.

### 6. Record income that is not a booking
1. Open **Revenue** and tap **Add revenue**.
2. Set the **Date**, choose the **Type / source**, type a **Description** and the **Amount**.
3. Tap **Save revenue**.

### 7. Fix a wrong booking amount in revenue
1. Do not look for an edit button on the Revenue screen. Booking lines are locked there.
2. Open **Guests**, edit the booking and correct the **Total stay**.
3. Tap **Save booking**. The revenue line follows.

### 8. See how full we are next month
1. Open **Occupancy**.
2. Tap the right arrow (**Next month**).
3. Read the **Occupancy** card and look at the calendar for free rooms.

### 9. See who owes us money
1. Open **Operations** and tap the **Revenue & collection** tab.
2. Read the **Outstanding balances** list. The biggest debt is first.
3. Tick **Include older** to add guests who left more than 90 days ago.

### 10. Month-end: give the accountant the legal report
1. During the month, tick **Share with legal** on the expenses and **Show on legal page** on the bookings the accountant should see (and **Show to legal** on manual revenue).
2. Open **Legal & accounting**.
3. Choose the year and the month.
4. Tap **CSV** for a spreadsheet file, or **Print** to print it or save it as a PDF.
5. Send the file to the accountant in your usual way. The app does not send it for you.

### 11. Review an expense as the lawyer or accountant
1. Open **Legal & accounting**.
2. Find the expense. Open its details to see the receipt.
3. Tap the **Accept**, **Needs clarification** or **Reject** icon.
4. Add a note if needed and tap **Save review**.

### 12. Correct an account balance after counting the cash
1. Open **Accounts**.
2. Use **Adjust balance** on the account.
3. Choose **Add money (+)** or **Remove money (−)**, type the difference as the **Amount**, add a **Description**.
4. Tap **Post adjustment**.

### 13. Add a new team member
1. Open **Settings**, then **People**.
2. Tap **Invite people**.
3. Type their **Email**, choose their **Role** and tap **Create invitation**.
4. Tap **Copy** to copy the **Invitation link**, or tap **Open in your email app**.
5. Send the link to the person yourself. It works for 7 days.

### 14. Find expenses that still need a receipt
1. Open **Overview** and look at the **No invoice / receipt** card.
2. Tap it to open **Expenses**.
3. Edit each expense that has no paperclip and attach the receipt under **Receipt or invoice**.

### 15. Look at the figures in another currency
1. An Owner adds the currency once: **Settings**, **Organisation**, **Currencies**, tap the code under **Display currencies**, **Save changes**.
2. After that, anyone can tap the currency code in the sidebar (computer) or top bar (phone).

### 16. Look up a stored password
1. Open **Log-in vault**.
2. Find the service and tap **Reveal**.
3. Tap the copy icon (**Copy password**), or the eye icon to read it.

## Questions and troubleshooting

### Signing in

**I forgot my password.**
Password reset by email is not available at the moment. The **Forgot?** link on the sign-in screen explains this. You have two options:
- If you are still signed in on another phone or computer, open **Settings**, **Profile**, **Change password** there. You need your current password to do this.
- Otherwise, ask the person who runs the service for you to set a new password.

**I did not get a confirmation email after signing up.**
None is sent. Your account works straight away. Just sign in.

**It says "Wrong email or password."**
Check for a typing mistake and for capital letters in the password. Tap the eye icon to see what you typed. The email address is not sensitive to capital letters.

**It says "Too many attempts."**
Wait a minute and try again.

**I signed in and the app asks me to set up a workspace, but my company already has one.**
You have an account but you are not a member of your company's organisation yet (or your access was suspended). Do not create a new workspace. Ask an Owner to send you an invitation link, or to switch your access back on under **Settings**, **Team**.

### Invitations

**The invitation link says it can no longer be used.**
Invitation links work for 7 days and only once. If someone invited the same email again, the older link stops working too. Ask for a new link. In **Settings**, **Team**, the person who invited you can use **Resend (new link)**.

**It says I am signed in with a different email.**
The invitation only works for the email it was made for. Sign out, open the link again, and sign in or create an account with that email.

**Nobody received my invitation.**
The app does not send invitation emails. After you tap **Create invitation** you must copy the link and send it yourself, or use **Open in your email app**.

### Things I cannot see or do

**A screen is missing from my menu.**
Your role does not allow you to see it. Ask an Owner. See [Roles and who can see what](#roles-and-who-can-see-what).

**I can see a list but there is no Add button, pencil or bin.**
Your role may view that screen but not add, change or delete there.

**I opened a link to a screen and landed somewhere else.**
When your role may not see a screen, the app quietly takes you to the first screen you are allowed to see.

**The edit and delete buttons on a revenue line are greyed out.**
That line comes from a booking. Change the booking on the **Guests** screen instead.

**I cannot switch property.**
The property name can only be tapped when you have more than one active property, or belong to more than one organisation.

### Money and numbers

**The amounts look wrong after I changed the currency.**
- The currency switch only changes how amounts are displayed. Your records are not changed. Tap the base currency to see the stored amounts.
- Converted amounts use today's exchange rates, so they move a little from day to day.
- Look at the dot next to the currency codes. If it is amber, the rates are old or missing. Tap it to refresh. If the app has no rate at all for a currency, amounts can show with the new currency name but the old number until rates load.
- Forms and exported files always use the base currency.

**Revenue is higher than the money we actually received.**
Revenue counts the full **Total stay** of each booking, dated on the check-out day, even when the guest has not paid everything. Money still owed is shown as **Outstanding**.

**A booking is missing from Revenue.**
Open the booking and check that **Add to revenue** is ticked. Also remember the revenue line is dated on the check-out day, so it may be in a later month than you expect.

**A booking or expense is missing from the Legal & accounting screen.**
Check the tick box on the record: **Show on legal page** (booking), **Share with legal** (expense) or **Show to legal** (manual revenue). Then check the year and month chosen on the Legal screen.

**An expense shows "no rate" and is not in the totals.**
It was saved in a foreign currency before the app had an exchange rate for it. Tap the dot next to the currency switch to refresh the rates, then open the expense and save it again.

**An account balance did not change after I recorded a payment or expense.**
The balance only moves when the **Payment method** you chose is linked to an account. Check the link under **Settings**, **Payments**. With **Not set**, or a method with no account (such as **Other**), nothing moves.

**The account balance in the app does not match the real cash.**
Use **Adjust balance** on the **Accounts** screen to add or remove the difference, with a note explaining why.

**Occupancy looks too low or too high.**
Occupancy counts active rooms only. Check on the **Rooms** screen that closed rooms are switched off and that you have no rooms that do not exist. Also check for bookings entered twice.

**The Overview and the Partner dashboard do not agree for my second property.**
Money figures (revenue, expenses, capital, accounts) cover the whole organisation. Bookings, rooms and occupancy are for the property you have selected.

### Invoices

**The invoice does not show the last payment or the new price.**
An invoice is a copy of the booking at the moment the invoice was first created, and it cannot be refreshed from the app at present. Record all payments before creating the invoice.

**The guest cannot open the invoice.**
Check that you sent the complete link. The link works without signing in. If invoices were removed with **Reset data**, old links stop working.

**Nothing happens when I tap Email invoice.**
The app opens the email program set up on your device. If there is none, nothing opens. Create the invoice instead, copy the web address of the invoice tab and paste it into a message.

**My company details are wrong on the invoice.**
An Owner changes them under **Settings**, **Invoices**. Invoices that already exist keep the details they were created with.

### Receipts and files

**Receipt scan says it is "not configured".**
Receipt scanning needs a paid reading service that the person who runs the app for you has to switch on. Until then, add the expense by hand and attach the photo with **Choose file** or **Camera**.

**Receipt scan says "Too many scans."**
There is a limit of 60 scans per hour for each person. Wait a while, or type the expense in.

**The scan filled in the wrong amount or date.**
The reading is automatic and not always right. Correct the fields before you save.

**I scanned a receipt but the expense has no attachment.**
Scanning does not keep the photo. You must tap **Attach this image** before saving. Edit the expense and attach the photo under **Receipt or invoice**.

**"File is larger than 10 MB" or "File type not allowed".**
One file can be at most 10 MB. Take the photo at a lower quality, or save the document as a smaller PDF. Program files and unusual formats are not accepted.

**Export does nothing, or Print / PDF does not open.**
**Export** is greyed out when the list is empty. **Print / PDF** opens a new tab, so allow pop-ups for the app in your browser.

### Log-in vault

**The vault says it is locked, or that the key is missing.**
The vault scrambles passwords with a secret key before storing them. That key has to be set once by whoever runs the service for you. Until it is set, you cannot add, edit or reveal passwords. You can still read the list of services and user names. Pass the message shown on the screen to that person. There is nothing you can fix yourself.

**The password disappeared while I was looking at it.**
A revealed password is hidden again after 30 seconds for safety. Tap **Reveal** again.

### The app itself

**The app looks broken or has no colours.**
Your browser is too old. Update Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox to a current version.

**I do not see the Install app option.**
On Android use Chrome. On iPhone and iPad use Safari, and look under the **Share** button for **Add to Home Screen**.

**Does the app work without internet?**
No. It needs a connection to load and to save.

**Can guests pay online, or can I pay my subscription by card in the app?**
No. The app does not take online payments. Guest payments are recorded by hand on the booking. The **Billing** page in Settings only shows the plans and opens an email to ask about them. Nothing is charged automatically.

**Does the app send emails or text messages?**
No. It does not send invoices, invitations, reminders or password resets by itself. Where a screen offers an email (invoice, invitation), it opens a ready-written message in your own email app for you to send.

**I deleted something by mistake.**
Deleting cannot be undone from the app. An Owner can see what was changed and by whom in the **Audit log** under **Settings**, **Data**, but the record has to be typed in again.
