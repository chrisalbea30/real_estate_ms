Presenter's script

Sojourn Demo Tour

One continuous walk through the whole app. Each stop gives you the screen to open, what to say, and exactly what to click. Tick a stop when you finish it.

38 stops97 min full tour15 min short versionSign in as admin@seaview.com

Before you start

Do these before the audience arrives.

  1. Regenerate the demo data on the day, so that "today" looks right (arrivals, departures, in-house guests, expiring fire extinguishers). Open a terminal in the project folder and run:
    node scripts/seed-demo.mjs "Seaview Resort Co." --reset

    It wipes and refills only that workspace's demo data. Team, roles, rooms and settings stay.

  2. Sign in at /login as admin@seaview.com. You are the Owner of "Seaview Resort Co.", property "Seaview Resort", with Room 1 to Room 5.
  3. Have a receipt photo ready on the device you present from (a clear photo of any shop receipt, as a picture file, not a PDF). Try Scan receipt once before the demo. If it answers that receipt scanning "is not switched on for this service yet", the scanning service is not switched on; use the by-hand steps in section 11 instead and say so honestly.
  4. Check the Log-in vault at /app/logins: it should list 5 log-ins and show no "vault is locked" box.
  5. Decide the currency. The figures are kept in THB. The switch at the bottom of the sidebar also offers EUR and USD. Pick the one your audience thinks in and tap the small dot next to the codes so it is green (fresh rates).
  6. Open the links in one browser window, maximised, in the light theme. Allow pop-ups for the site (Print / PDF and invoices open new tabs). Keep one private (incognito) window ready for sections 2, 7 and 35.
  7. Know what the app does not do, and never claim otherwise: it sends no email by itself, online payment is not connected, and password reset by email is not available. See Appendix B.

What is in the demo workspace: 271 bookings from January to November, several guests in-house tonight, 12 invoices (INV-2026-0001 to INV-2026-0012), 136 expenses, 33 manual revenue lines, Bank and Cash accounts, 4 partners, a budget for this year, 18 tasks, 10 complaints, about 295 staff-hours entries, 11 documents and 5 vault log-ins. Guest names change every time the data is regenerated, so this script describes guests ("the in-house guest with a red balance") instead of naming them. Each room has two example photos. Attachments open a placeholder picture (the app logo).

Records you add live all start with the word "Demo" so they are easy to find and delete. Each section says when to clean up. If you forget, regenerating the data removes them.

Short version (15 minutes): sections 5, 7 (skip the email step), 10, 11 (scan only), 13 and 15 (first and fourth tab only).

Public landing page

What a new customer sees: features, how it works, mobile, pricing, FAQ.

3 min Open screen /
Say

This is what a new customer sees first. It explains the product in one page and lets them start a free trial without talking to anyone.

Do

  1. Read the headline, then point at Start free trial and See how it works, and the line "14-day trial · No card required · Cancel anytime".
  2. In the top menu click Features. Show the six cards (bookings and invoices, receipts scanned into expenses, one revenue table, legal review, budgets and partner reporting, day-to-day operations).
  3. Click How it works. Read the four steps "Guest → Revenue → Legal → Partners". This is the idea of the whole demo: a booking is typed once.
  4. Click Mobile. Point at "Installs like an app", "Camera-first receipts", "Exports anywhere" and "Nothing lost". Scroll a little to the dark "Built to be trusted" block (data isolation, roles, encrypted vault, audit log).
  5. Click Pricing. Flip the Monthly / Yearly switch. Show Starter, Growth and Enterprise (Talk to us).
  6. Click FAQ and open one question, for example "Is this a booking engine or channel manager?" (the honest answer is no; it is the back office).
  7. Click the sun/moon icon in the top bar once to show the page also has a dark theme, then click it until it is light again.

Do add a booking live

  1. Tap Add booking. In New booking, under Stay: Room "Room 4 (No. 4)" (free tonight in fresh demo data), Guest name "Demo Guest", Check-in today, Check-out tomorrow, Booking source "Direct Booking", Guests 2.
  2. Under Pricing: Rate per night 3500. Total stay fills in by itself.

  4. Under Contact & immigration: Email "demo.guest@example.com". Leave TM30 immigration notification filed unticked.
  5. Under Reporting: leave Add to revenue ticked and tick Show on legal page. Explain the two tick boxes: one makes it income, the other shows it to the lawyer or accountant.

Do split payment, invoice, email

  1. Search "Demo Guest". Point at the labels In-House, TM30 pending, Revenue, Legal.
  2. Tap the pencil icon (Edit). Fill Payment 2 date today and Payment 2 amount 2500, tick TM30 immigration notification filed, and tap Save booking. The balance is now zero.

  4. On the invoice tap Print / Save PDF and show the printer window ("Save as PDF"), then cancel it.
  5. Copy the invoice address and paste it into the private window: it opens without signing in. Say: this is the link the guest receives, so send it only to the guest.
  6. Back on Guests, tap the envelope icon (Email invoice). Your own email app opens with the address, the subject and the link already written. Say honestly: the app prepares the message; you press send. Close the draft.
  7. Say: the invoice is a copy of the booking at the moment it was created, so record all payments first. Also: the app does not block two bookings in the same room on the same dates; the Occupancy calendar makes an overlap visible.

Appendix B. If they ask

Short, honest answers.

Does it send emails? No. The app sends no invoices, invitations, reminders or password resets by itself. For an invoice or an invitation it opens a ready-written message in your own email app, or gives you a link to copy, and you send it.

Can guests pay online? Can I pay my subscription by card? Not today. Online payment is not connected. Guest payments are recorded by hand on the booking. The Billing page shows the plans and opens an email to us; nothing is charged automatically.

What if someone forgets their password? Password reset by email is not available yet. Someone who is still signed in on another device changes it under Settings, Profile. Otherwise the person who runs the service sets a new password for them.

Does it stop double bookings? No. The app does not block two bookings in the same room on the same dates. The Occupancy calendar draws overlapping bookings one above the other, so they are easy to spot.

If I change a booking, does the invoice change? No. An invoice is a copy of the booking at the moment it was first created, and there is no button yet to make a fresh one. Record all payments first, then create the invoice.

Does receipt scanning always work? It needs a paid reading service (an AI key) that the operator of the app switches on. Without it the screen says scanning "is not switched on for this service yet" and you add the expense by hand and attach the photo. When it is on, it reads photos, not PDFs, and it can make mistakes, so always check the amount and date. The limit is 60 scans per hour per person.

What happens when the 14-day trial ends? At the moment nothing is locked or limited. The date is shown for information. Plans and prices are arranged by email.

Can other customers see my data? No. Every record belongs to one organisation and the database itself keeps organisations apart. Files are private and opened through short-lived links. Stored log-ins are encrypted. Inside your organisation, roles decide who sees which screen.

Does it work offline? No. It needs an internet connection to load and to save, also when installed on a phone.

Is it a booking engine or channel manager? No. It is the back office. You record bookings that arrive from your channels or directly, and the app turns them into occupancy, revenue, invoices and reports.

Can I get my data out? Yes. Every list has an Export menu, and Settings, Data downloads every list as Excel, CSV or JSON. Attached files stay in the app and are opened from each record.

Can something deleted be brought back? No. Deleting cannot be undone. The Audit log shows what was changed and by whom, but the record has to be typed in again.
